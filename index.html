Haptic lab

Shape library

Every input. Mouse: drag, throw, click, double-click for a shockwave, right-click to recolor, wheel to resize, hover to nudge. Touch: drag, throw, tap, double-tap, pinch a shape, hold and release to pop. Pen pressure squashes shapes. Keys: Space, S, M, R, G, B, 1 to 8, arrows. Gamepad: left stick tilts gravity, A spawns, B shakes, X morphs, Y booms. Phone: tilt, shake, sound, vibration. Camera: wave. Voice: say a command. Scrolling the page jostles the shapes. Haptics use the Vibration API on Android, a Safari switch trick on iOS 17.4 and later, and gamepad rumble. Sensors need HTTPS. The Pixel 4’s Motion Sense radar isn’t exposed to websites, so camera wave is the stand-in.
