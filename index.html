Haptic lab

Shape library

Haptics use the Vibration API on Android, a Safari switch trick on iOS 17.4 and later, and gamepad rumble where available. Tilt and shake use the accelerometer and gyroscope, so they need HTTPS (GitHub Pages works). The Pixel 4’s Motion Sense radar isn’t exposed to websites. Keys: Space spawns, S shakes, arrows nudge. Long-press a shape to pop it.
